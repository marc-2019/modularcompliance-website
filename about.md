# About Modular Compliance

> Modular Compliance, by Instilligent Limited (NZ). NZ-built compliance register and workflow tool that maps legislation into structured frameworks, controls and tasks. Day-to-day compliance work is done by people; the tool keeps the record.

## What it is

Modular Compliance is NZ-built compliance register and workflow software for small and medium New Zealand businesses. It maps legislation into structured frameworks, controls and tasks, records who completed what and when, stores supporting evidence, and keeps an auditable record. Day-to-day compliance work is done by people; the tool keeps the record.

## What Modular Compliance is not

Modular Compliance is a register and workflow tool, not an automated compliance platform. It does not connect to operational systems, monitor transactions, screen activity, or take regulatory action on the customer's behalf. It is not a certification body, does not provide legal advice, and does not notify regulators for you. See [what it doesn't do](https://modularcompliance.com/#boundaries) on the homepage for the full list.
