2. What Modular Compliance is

Modular Compliance is a compliance register and workflow tool. It maps legislation into structured frameworks, controls and tasks, records who completed what and when, stores supporting evidence, and keeps an auditable record. Day-to-day compliance work is done by your people; Modular Compliance keeps the record.

Modular Compliance does not connect to your operational systems, monitor transactions, screen activity, or take regulatory action on your behalf. It does not provide legal advice, does not certify compliance with any law or standard, and does not notify regulators (including WorkSafe or the Privacy Commissioner) on your behalf.
