Modular Compliance / Security

Security

This page states only what is factually true about Modular Compliance today. We do not claim certifications, penetration-test results, or audit outcomes we have not obtained. If a claim isn't listed here, treat it as not yet true.

Encryption in transit

The marketing site and the app are served over HTTPS with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) enabled. Traffic between your browser and Modular Compliance is encrypted in transit.

Access control

Modular Compliance uses role-based access control (RBAC) so you can restrict who can view, edit, or approve items in your compliance register. Enterprise plans support single sign-on via SAML 2.0 and OIDC, so login can be managed through your own identity provider.

Audit trail

Task completions and policy/framework terms acceptances are recorded with an audit trail — who did what, and when — as part of your compliance register.

Evidence & document storage

Supporting evidence and documents you upload are stored alongside the tasks and frameworks they relate to, with access controlled by the same role-based permissions as the rest of your register.

What we don't claim

Modular Compliance does not hold ISO 27001, SOC 2, or any other third-party security certification today, and this page does not claim otherwise. Customers can build ISO 27001 or SOC 2 as a custom framework inside their own register — that is a template for the customer's own compliance work, not a certification of Instilligent's own infrastructure, and not automated evidence collection.

Modular Compliance does not monitor your operational systems, connect to your infrastructure, or automatically collect security evidence from integrations. See what it doesn't do on the homepage.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a security issue, email info@instilligent.com with details. We don't run a public bug-bounty programme today.

For how we handle personal data, see our privacy policy.
