IPP 1: Purpose of collection

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 1

Personal information should only be collected for a lawful purpose connected with your functions.

IPP 2: Source of personal information

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 2

Collect personal information from the individual concerned where practicable.

IPP 3: What to tell people when collecting

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 3

When collecting, take reasonable steps so people know who you are, why you collect, and what happens to their information.

IPP 4: Manner of collection

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 4

Collection must be lawful, fair, and not unreasonably intrusive.

IPP 5: Storage and security

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 5

Take reasonable steps to protect personal information against loss, access, use, modification or disclosure.

IPP 6: Access

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 6

People can request access to their personal information you hold.

IPP 7: Correction

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 7

People can request correction of their personal information.

IPP 8: Accuracy

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 8

Before using personal information, take reasonable steps to ensure it is accurate, up to date, complete, relevant, and not misleading.

IPP 9: Retention

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 9

Do not keep personal information longer than required for the purposes it may lawfully be used.

IPP 10: Use limits

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 10

Use personal information only for the purpose it was obtained, or related purposes / permitted exceptions.

IPP 11: Disclosure limits

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 11

Do not disclose personal information except for the purpose obtained or under permitted exceptions.

IPP 12: Disclosure outside New Zealand

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 12

Special rules apply when disclosing personal information outside NZ.

IPP 13: Unique identifiers

Privacy Act 2020 s22 IPP 13

Limits on assigning and requiring unique identifiers (e.g. IRD numbers).
